Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Waterfall Tools viewer at waterfall-tools.com and the capture tool at capture.waterfall-tools.com, together with the capture tool's browser extension and command-line helper. They are open-source software from the Waterfall Tools project. In this policy, "we" means the project.

The short version: both sites run in your browser. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not keep your test results or the files you open. Data leaves your device only when you ask for it to, and it goes only where you choose.

What stays on your device

  • Files you open in the viewer are read and processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to us.
  • History. The viewer keeps a list of results you have opened, and copies of them, in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB). You can clear it from the History view or by clearing site data for waterfall-tools.com in your browser.
  • Test results made with the capture tool are created on your own computer or phone and stay there until you download, publish or delete them.
  • Settings and storage-provider credentials, such as a bucket configuration or a sign-in token, are kept in your browser and sent only to that provider.

When data leaves your device

  • Loading a file by URL. When you give the viewer a URL, your browser may fetch it through our proxy at waterfall-tools.com/fetch, which adds the permission headers browsers need. The proxy passes the file straight through to your browser and does not store it. It forwards your IP address to the site the file comes from, and briefly counts failed requests per IP address, in memory only, to limit abuse.
  • Perfetto. If you open a trace in the Perfetto tab, the trace is handed, inside your browser, to the Perfetto UI served by ui.perfetto.dev. That page is run by Google, and its own terms apply.
  • Publishing and uploads. When you choose to upload or publish a result, it goes to the storage you pick: Google Drive, an S3-compatible bucket, Dropbox, or a plain download. It is sent directly from your browser to that provider, under your account, and that provider's terms apply. Anyone you give a published link to can open that result.
  • Running a test loads the page you asked to test, from the device running the test, as any browser would.
  • Hosting. The sites are delivered through Cloudflare and a web server we run. Like any website, they receive the usual request details: your IP address, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent. These may appear in standard server and Cloudflare logs, which we use only to run and secure the sites.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive to the capture tool, we ask only for the drive.file permission. It lets the tool see and manage only the files it creates in your Drive, not the rest of your Drive. The tool uses it to upload results you choose to save. If you choose to publish a result, the tool also sets that one file so anyone with the link can view it. The sign-in token stays in your browser and is never sent to us. We do not read, keep, sell or share your Google data.

Waterfall Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the tool's access at any time from your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services. Files it already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Children

The tools are meant for web developers and are not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
